About the Collection

Tide

Salt on fabric. Wind in the seams. Sand inside pockets. Rust on metal. Memories that refuse to disappear. TIDE MEMORY explores garments as objects shaped by time, weather, and movement.

Relocation

New shores, same North Star.

Hamvcl is expanding its creative practice into Europe while continuing to operate in Vietnam. The move is one of scale and access — not of identity. The brand's craft, references, and people remain rooted in Vietnam, even as its reach grows.
